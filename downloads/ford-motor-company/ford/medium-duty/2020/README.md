# Ford Medium Duty 2020

## Important: No MY2020 F-650/F-750

Ford did **not produce a 2020 model year** for the Medium Duty F-650 and F-750. The 2020 model year was explicitly replaced by the 2021 model year, so the model-year sequence jumps from **2019 directly to 2021**.

Ford's 2021 F-650/F-750 order guide states that MY2020 would not be produced and that this change was made to align the Medium Duty product with Model Year / Calendar Year market expectations. Ford therefore did not have a MY2020 F-650/F-750 lineup to document in this folder.

This folder is retained to document the missing model year and should **not** be interpreted as evidence that a 2020 F-650 or F-750 was produced.

Source: Ford 2021 MY Medium Duty F-650/F-750 Order Guide, revised September 20, 2019.
